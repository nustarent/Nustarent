// WHO WE ARE

About

Photos by Rayden ✦ The Goat

Nobody planned for NUSTAR to be a thing. It just kind of happened.

Freshman year, 2019. A group of five kids found out their school had a music studio sitting there after hours and figured why not. They called themselves Dentes Country. Honestly it was more for fun than anything. They were being lazy, goofing around, and it never really went anywhere. But it started something.

That same year Nate and Wyolkens linked up. They clicked over music right away, and from the jump they both knew they wanted to cook together at some point.

Dentes Country fell apart but Nate kept going. He tried again with a group called Troubled Youth, same story. Then came Origins, different crew, same energy. Then COVID hit and killed the momentum before it could even get going.

Fast forward to 2023. Wyolkens was on a college campus, stumbled on a music studio, and the first person he thought of was Nate. No strategy behind it. That's his boy, and Nate loves music more than anybody he knows. Nate had moved away sophomore year but they stayed in touch the whole time. He made the call, Nate was in, and they locked in.

No engineer. No real plan. They booked sessions anyway thinking they'd figure it out on the fly. They didn't. Nobody came through to help and nothing got recorded. Even after all that, neither of them walked away from it.

In 2024 Nate put out a solo instrumental project as WACKO PROPHET called THE PARTY ENDED AN HOUR AGO. Just beats, no features, no lyrics. The college studio thing never worked out but this did, and it kicked things off for real.

2025. Six years after that first after school session, Nate still had the same vision. Wyolkens said enough doing it alone, let's actually lock in. They found a studio called Dumblit through a random search, booked real sessions, and got to work.

Getting the album together wasn't smooth. They needed female vocalists, so both of them were hitting up everyone they knew. Three women came through. One confirmed she was in and then ghosted Nate out of nowhere. But the ones who showed up delivered, and the album got finished.

First single New Egypt dropped October 3rd, 2025. The full album LAFAYETTE came out October 24th, 2025.

UNISEX is next, coming soon. And they're just getting started.

Special thanks to Rayden ✦ the camera man. The goat.

// THE ARTIST

Wacko Prophet

// In His Own Words
How would you describe your sound?

Progressive. I'm always looking for a new sound, a new way to explore and flesh out concepts in a satisfying way. That's what keeps me going, especially when it comes to producing. I love this shit.

Who influenced you?

Kendrick Lamar, Tyler the Creator, Childish Gambino. KAYTRANADA is my favorite and most recent influence, shout out to him. And of course the greats: Neptunes, Nas, Wu-Tang, and 2Pac. He's the reason I started music in the first place.

What do you want people to feel?

Vulnerable and free. Goosebumps when you hear those glittery chords, the punchy bass, the swift drums. I want people to feel authenticity, but also reality.

What separates you from other artists?

I don't just strive for originality, I advocate for preserving the past and learning from it. I truly feel like a hip hop historian. Over 50 years of influence guides me into the future.

What would people not expect about you?

That I'm multimedia. Music isn't my only passion, I love to direct and write. I can't wait to show that side of me. It's honestly the most passionate thing I hold.

Where are you from?

Jersey born and raised. I always moved around, especially through middle and high school. Those experiences shaped who I am. As much as it affected me, I learned to appreciate it. Plus Jersey needs more representation. On God.

// THE MANAGER

Wyolkens

// The Man Behind It
Who is Wyolkens?

Ocean Township, NJ. He's not doing this for a title or to say he manages somebody. Nate is his boy, he's talented, and Wyolkens wanted to be there for it.

How it started

They met in high school and he just thought Nate was cool, that's literally it. Years later he found a studio on a college campus and the first person he thought of was Nate. He just knew Nate loved music more than anyone and deserved a real shot at it.

What he does

He checks in on progress, keeps the business side moving, and makes sure there's an actual plan behind the music. Hardest part? Sometimes he doesn't agree with how things go and has to let the artist do it their way anyway. That's part of the job.

Outside of NUSTAR

Big anime guy. One Piece, Dragon Ball Z, Trigun, grew up on all of it. He also makes his own music as W.Y.O, nothing too serious, just for fun. End goal is bigger than all that though. When NUSTAR makes it, the point is to take care of family, look out for the people who were there from the jump, and keep making music that means something. The Grammy is coming.
